# Practice

My attempts, not site code. Nothing in here is imported by the app.

The pattern: Claude builds the real thing properly, then I write my own version
of it in here without looking, and we compare. If mine turns out to be at least
as good and I can explain it, it gets swapped into the site and this copy stays
as the record.

Writing my version of something that already works is the point. I'm not
guessing in a vacuum, I'm finding out exactly where mine diverges from something
that does the job.
